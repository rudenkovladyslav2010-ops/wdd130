Our Trips

Family River Escape

Difficulty: Class I

Duration: 2–4 hours

Minimum age: 5

Price: Children $40; Adults $60

Best for: Families and complete beginners

Experience: Calm and relaxing rafting through wide, clear, open channels

Included: Experienced river guide, raft and paddles, life jackets, helmets, safety briefing, and bottled water

Whitewater Challenge

Difficulty: Class III

Duration: 3–5 hours

Minimum age: 12

Price: Children $60; Adults $80

Best for: Intermediate rafters with good paddling skills and reasonable physical fitness

Experience: True whitewater with moderate, irregular waves, stronger currents, and more technical maneuvering than the beginner trip

Included: Experienced river guide, raft and paddles, life jackets, helmets, safety briefing, and bottled water

Ultimate Whitewater Challenge

Difficulty: Class V

Duration: 5–7 hours

Minimum age: 18

Price: $100

Best for: Highly experienced whitewater rafters with advanced technical skills and excellent physical fitness

Experience: Extreme whitewater with powerful rapids, steep drops, large waves, and difficult technical maneuvers

Included: Experienced river guide, raft and paddles, life jackets, helmets, safety briefing, and bottled water
